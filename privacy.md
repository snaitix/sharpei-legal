# Privacy Policy

**App:** Shar Pei
**Effective date:** May 29, 2026
**Last updated:** May 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shar Pei ("the App", "we", "us") handles
information when you use the App on your iOS device.

## Summary

- We do **not** collect, store, or transmit personal data to our own servers.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- We do **not** create user accounts.
- We have **no servers**: the App has no backend of any kind.
- All information the App shows is processed and stored **locally** on your
  device.
- The App makes direct connections to a few public endpoints solely to measure
  network reachability and latency. See *Network checks* below.

## Information the App processes

### 1. Network configuration (on-device only)

The App reads information about the network your device is currently connected
to, including:

- the type of connection (Wi-Fi, cellular, wired);
- the Wi-Fi network name (SSID), when permitted by iOS;
- IP addresses assigned to your device;
- DNS server configuration;
- the default gateway (router) address;
- the list of active network interfaces.

This information is read and used **only on your device** to display the network
overview and details. It is never sent to us.

### 2. Network checks (public endpoints)

To measure reachability, latency, and stability, the App opens short TCP
connections from your device to publicly available endpoints — by default the
public DNS resolvers operated by Cloudflare (1.1.1.1), Google (8.8.8.8), and
Quad9 (9.9.9.9). These connections are necessary for the diagnostics feature and
are made **directly from your device** — they do not pass through any
infrastructure operated by us (we operate none).

As part of normal internet operation, those public services may observe the IP
address of the connection. We do not control them and we receive no information
from them. Please consult their own privacy policies if you wish to learn how
they handle such data.

### 3. Local event log

The App keeps a small log of network events (for example, going online or
offline, a change of connection type, or a completed diagnostics run) so you can
review recent activity. This log is stored **only on your device** and you can
clear it at any time from within the App. It is never transmitted.

### 4. iOS system permissions

The App requests a single permission:

- **Location (While Using the App).** iOS requires an app to hold location
  permission before it is allowed to read the current Wi-Fi network name (SSID).
  The App uses this permission **only** to read the network name. It does
  **not** determine, record, or share your geographic position or coordinates,
  and it does not track your location.

You may revoke this permission at any time in iOS Settings, and the App will
continue to work without showing the Wi-Fi name.

## Data we do not collect

We do not collect, and the App does not transmit to us, any of the following:

- name, email address, phone number, or other contact details;
- device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV) for advertising or tracking purposes;
- your geographic location or coordinates;
- contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health data;
- crash logs or diagnostic data delivered to us (Apple may collect aggregated
  crash and usage data under your iOS privacy settings; we do not receive it).

## Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly process data
from children.

## Data retention and deletion

Because all data the App uses lives only on your device, deleting the App removes
it entirely. There is no server-side data for us to delete.

## Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to
access, correct, export, or erase. If you nevertheless wish to contact us about
your privacy, see *Contact* below.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be
reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
